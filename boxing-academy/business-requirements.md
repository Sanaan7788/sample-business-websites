# Business Requirements

## Business Goal
Build a modern boxing academy website that:
- Generates leads
- Converts visitors into free trial bookings
- Builds trust
- Improves local SEO
- Allows easy content management

## Primary Actions
1. Book Free Trial
2. View Class Schedule
3. Call the Gym
4. Get Directions
5. Become a Member

## Target Audience
- Complete beginners
- Fitness enthusiasts
- Competitive fighters
- Parents looking for youth boxing
- Women interested in boxing fitness
- People searching for weight loss and conditioning

## Important Features
- Free Trial Booking
- Dynamic Class Schedule
- Membership Pricing
- Programs Page
- Coaches Page
- Testimonials
- Success Stories
- Gallery
- Blog
- Contact Page
- Mobile Sticky CTA
- Google Reviews Integration
- CMS for content management

## SEO Goals
- Rank locally for:
  - Boxing Gym <City>
  - Boxing Classes <City>
  - Beginner Boxing <City>
  - Kids Boxing <City>
  - Personal Boxing Training <City>

## Non-Goals
- No merchandise store initially
- No online classes initially
- No member portal initially
- No complex payment system initially
